Support for the island guide

How to get help with the Koh Tao Climbing iOS app — a community climbing guide for Koh Tao, not a generic worldwide guidebook.

What this app is. An original, offline community guide to climbing on Koh Tao, Thailand: crags, routes, topos, photos, trip reports, ferry and season notes, and a bundled map of Koh Tao and Nang Yuan.

It is not an account product, not a social network, and not a white-label template reused for other islands.

How to get help

  1. Open an issue on the public repository: github.com/capyreadonly/koh-tao-climbing/issues. Include the app version (currently 0.1) and what you were looking at (crag name, route name, or Plan screen).
  2. The same database is on the web guide: capyreadonly.github.io/koh-tao-climbing. Use that if you want to compare a fact against the published site.

There is no in-app chat and no user account to recover. The guide is on your device once the app is installed.

Climbing and access questions

This project compiles public sources; it is not a guiding company. For live access, bolting, or courses on the island, use the operators listed inside the app’s Plan → Services screen (Goodtime Adventures, The Bunker, Koh Tao Climbing Club, and others as documented there). Grades and access change — treat the guide as a starting point, not a guarantee.

Privacy

The iOS app does not collect personal data. See the privacy page.

App identity

Name on the Home Screen: Koh Tao Climbing. Bundle ID: com.kohtaoclimbing.guide. Team: a community compilation published from the capyreadonly/koh-tao-climbing repository.
